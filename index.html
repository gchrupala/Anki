How the cards are scheduled

Each fact is a flash card scheduled with Anki's classic algorithm (SM-2, Anki's default settings). Your child types the answer and the app presses the Anki button for them:

AnswerAnki button
WrongAgain: the fact comes back in 1 minute, or 10 minutes if it had been learned before
Right, slower than 10 sHard
RightGood
Right within 4 sEasy, but only on a first showing or a review, not during the 1- and 10-minute steps

A new fact comes back after 1 and 10 minutes, then the next day. After that, every right answer multiplies the gap by about 2.5.
