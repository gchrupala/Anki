Target recall is the chance of remembering a fact when it comes up for review. Higher means more reviews.

How the cards are scheduled

Each fact is a flash card scheduled with FSRS (version 6, default parameters), the algorithm Anki offers alongside its older SM-2. FSRS estimates how likely your child is to remember each fact, and brings it back when that chance falls to the target recall.

Your child types the answer. It is checked as soon as it has as many digits as the right answer, or after 3 seconds without typing. The app then picks the Anki rating:

AnswerAnki rating
WrongAgain: the fact comes back in 1 minute, or 10 minutes if it had been learned before
Right, slower than 10 sHard
RightGood
Right within 4 sEasy, but only on a first showing or a review, not during the 1- and 10-minute steps

A new fact comes back after 1 and 10 minutes, then a few days later. Each time it is remembered the gap grows, faster for facts your child finds easy. New facts are introduced table by table, in a random order within each table.
